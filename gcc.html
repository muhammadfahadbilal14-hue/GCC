🇵🇰 PAKISTAN CRICKET

A legendary cricketing journey

Pakistan entered Test cricket in 1952 and developed a proud tradition of producing outstanding batsmen, fast bowlers, spinners and all-rounders.


Pakistan's greatest ODI World Cup achievement came in 1992. Led by Imran Khan, Pakistan defeated England in the final at Melbourne and became world champions.


Pakistan later won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup in 2009, defeating Sri Lanka in the final at Lord's. In 2017, Pakistan also won the ICC Champions Trophy, defeating India in the final.

1952 — Test Cricket Begins

Pakistan played its first Test against India in Delhi.

1992 — ODI World Champions

Imran Khan's Pakistan defeated England by 22 runs in the World Cup final.

2009 — T20 World Champions

Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by eight wickets in the T20 World Cup final.

2017 — Champions Trophy Winners

Pakistan defeated India by 180 runs in the Champions Trophy final.
